OT Segmentation Checker

Checks whether an OT network is segmented the way it should be. Load an Nmap scan, optionally add a Wireshark conversations export, and define your zones. The tool maps every host to a Purdue level, builds the zone-to-zone conduit matrix, and flags traffic that crosses a boundary it shouldn't.

Runs entirely in your browser · nothing is uploaded

1 · Inputs

Nmap scan recommended

XML output from nmap -sS -sV -O -oX scan.xml <subnets>. It tells the tool what each device is and which services it exposes.

Traffic conversations optional, strongly recommended

In Wireshark: Statistics → Conversations, open the TCP tab, then Copy → As CSV (repeat for UDP and paste below it). Shows who actually talks to whom, which is what segmentation is about.

Zones your intended design

One rule per line: subnet or IP = LEVEL name. Levels: L1 control (Level 0–1), L2 supervisory, L3 operations, DMZ, ENT enterprise/IT, SIS safety, PARTNER third-party/WAN. The most specific rule wins.

Your inputs are saved in this browser so you can come back to them.
